Image encode mode (bench rotation)

Pin a maximum-quality encoder for the tractor's image link. The automatic airtime-driven controller may demote below your choice if the link degrades, but never above it. Pick Auto to hand full control back to the airtime ladder. Pick Adaptive to enable per-tile heuristic selection that survives until the link goes critical, then falls back to the resilience floor.
